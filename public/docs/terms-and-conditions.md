CertiBot is a **compliance support tool**. Its purpose is to help organizations orient their compliance processes by providing AI-assisted analysis of normative standards and internal evidence. It is not a legal advisory service, a certification authority, or a substitute for professional auditing.

### Permitted uses

- Uploading internal evidence documents for automated analysis.
- Using the chatbot to explore and understand compliance requirements based on validated normative content.
- Reviewing AI-generated compliance assessments as part of an internal preparation process.
- Managing users and organizational data within the platform.

## 3. Subscription, Cancellation and Refund Policy

> **Note:** CertiBot is currently in an academic prototype phase. No commercial billing is active at this stage. The following policy applies to future production releases.

CertiBot operates on a **subscription-based model** with monthly payments. The subscription is defined entirely by the set of normative standards the organization selects. At registration, organizations must select at least one norm (e.g. ISO 27001, GDPR), and the monthly price is determined by the number and type of norms chosen. Norms can be added or removed at any time as the organization's compliance needs evolve.

### Removing a single norm

Organizations may remove an individual norm add-on at any time. The change takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no refund is issued for the remaining days of that period. Upon removal:

- Access to that norm's knowledge base and AI features (chatbot, compliance verification) is revoked.
- Existing audit results, conversation history, and uploaded evidence related to that norm remain accessible, as the overall subscription is still active.

### Full cancellation

Organizations may cancel their subscription entirely at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and no further charges are made. No refunds are issued for billing periods already paid. Full access to the platform remains available until the end of the paid period. After cancellation:

- **Immediately revoked:** AI chatbot, compliance verification engine, and access to all normative content knowledge bases.
- **30-day export window:** Organizations may request an export of their own data, including uploaded evidence documents, conversation history, chat messages, and compliance audit results (scores, per-criteria outcomes, and justifications), before it is permanently deleted.
